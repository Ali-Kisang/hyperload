# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus feedback on code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing secrets or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption of project spaces.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy. Report concerns privately using the contact method listed in the repository's SECURITY.md.
